The configuration window, sized for a thumb

Category tree, host and port, connection type and saved sessions with Load, Save and Delete. Everything sits where PuTTY always kept it, with touch targets you can hit on a phone at three in the morning.

What comes with it

  • Your own keys

    OpenSSH and PuTTY .ppk, Ed25519 and RSA. Passphrases are asked at login and never stored

  • Login the PuTTY way

    login as: and user@host's password: right in the terminal, asked again after Access denied

  • Telnet and Raw

    For switches, routers and the box under the desk, with window size and terminal type negotiation

  • Settings that do something

    Local echo, Backspace as ^H or ^?, bell, cursor shape, font, colour schemes and keepalives

  • Several sessions

    Keep more than one host open and switch between them from the taskbar

  • Nothing is logged

    No event log, no session logs, no analytics. Saved sessions hold settings, private keys stay in the iOS Keychain

Questions

Is this the official PuTTY?

No. It is an independent, unofficial app modelled on PuTTY by Simon Tatham. The code is new Swift, released under the MIT licence.

Why does my RSA key get refused?

RSA user keys are signed with SHA-1, which OpenSSH 8.8 and later reject by default. Use an Ed25519 key. RSA host keys and password logins are not affected.

Which login methods work?

Password and public key. Keyboard-interactive is not supported, so a server that offers only that method will not accept a password.

Why does a session drop in the background?

iOS suspends apps it cannot see. A connection survives a short switch of about 30 seconds; after that iOS closes it, and Restart Session brings it back.

What does the app keep on my phone?

Your saved sessions and cached host keys. Private keys sit in the iOS Keychain. Passwords, passphrases and everything you type are never written down.

Why are Rlogin and Serial greyed out?

iOS gives apps neither the privileged ports Rlogin needs nor serial lines. Encrypted .ppk version 3 keys are not read yet; version 2 keys are.
